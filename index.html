Learn from my readings

For each past meal eaten on its own (at least 15 g, no other carbs 4 hours either side, readings throughout), the app adds back the glucose the insulin removed. What is left is the rise from the carbs alone. It measures how long that rise took to reach 90% of its peak, which gives that meal's absorption time.

The time for each GI group is the middle value (median) of its meals, and only shown once there are 3 such meals. With the switch on, these replace the fixed times everywhere carbs on board is worked out. Basal insulin, exercise and sensor error aren't modelled, so treat them as a guide.

Subtract active insulin from corrections

Unlike the settings above, this one changes your suggested dose: when on, a correction is reduced by your current Active Insulin (IOB) — your meal dose is never affected. Off by default.

Lower the dose when glucose is below target

Like the one above, this changes your suggested dose. When on, a reading below your target takes insulin off the meal dose: (reading − target) ÷ correction factor, so 55 against a target of 100 and a factor of 50 takes off about 1 unit. It never takes the dose below zero. When off, a low reading adds nothing and takes nothing off. If you're actually low, treating the low comes first.
